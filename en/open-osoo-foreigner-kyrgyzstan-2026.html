Opening an LLC (OsOO) in Kyrgyzstan as a foreigner: what we know about registration

Who can be a founder, how long registration takes, what changed in November and what about the bank account. No state fee figure: the sources we checked do not give one.

Published November 19, 2026. Checked November 19, 2026.
Report a mistake
In short
  • Founders of an LLC can be foreign individuals and companies; there is no minimum capital.
  • Registration goes through a one-stop shop; the standard time is 3–5 working days, and online it can be faster.
  • New rules on company registration and liquidation (resolution No. 746) take effect on 20 November 2026.
  • We found neither the state fee nor the rules for a foreign sole proprietor in the sources we checked.
What changed
  • November 19, 2026First published.

Who can be a founder and is capital required

According to the National Investment Agency guide (data as of June 2026), founders of an LLC can be foreign individuals and legal entities. There can be no more than 30 founders. An LLC has no minimum charter capital, but it must be paid in full within the first year after registration.

How registration works and how long it takes

Registration follows a one-stop-shop principle: at the justice authority, tax service, statistics office and Social Fund at the same time, with a registration number, tax ID and statistical code assigned. The standard time is 3–5 working days, faster for an extra fee.

In July 2026 the head of the Chui-Bishkek justice department said that with internet access and an electronic or cloud signature a legal entity can be registered online. Express options take one working day, six hours or even three. Documents can be filed at any territorial justice office. There is no paper certificate any more; the document is electronic.

What changed in November

On 12 November 2026 Economist.kg reported that the rules for registering and liquidating companies had changed: resolution No. 746, signed on 9 November, takes effect on 20 November. When filing documents, foreigners may give not only a PIN but another identification number. We did not read the resolution itself, so before filing check that your documents meet the new requirements.

The bank account

Since 1 November 2026 a legal provision on a fee for accrediting accounts of foreign companies is in force; the government has not set the amount (see the news). A draft resolution proposes 200 thousand som and was put to public discussion on 28 October. The publication does not say the fee would apply to an LLC registered in Kyrgyzstan with a foreign founder: it concerns accounts of foreign companies. Ask your bank.

What we did not find

  • The state fee for registration.
  • Rules for a foreigner registering as a sole proprietor, and any residence-permit requirements.
  • Whether a foreign founder needs a visa or a residence permit: the sources we read give no direct requirement.
