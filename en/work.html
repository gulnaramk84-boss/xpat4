03 · Work & Study

Employment & Education

Work permits for foreigners, employment in KR, admission to Kyrgyz universities and student visas.

03.1

Work Permit

Foreigners employed by Kyrgyz companies must obtain a work permit, until 31 December 2026 the Ministry of Foreign Affairs issues the documents under a temporary procedure, then the functions move to the Ministry of Labour (guide). EAEU citizens (Russia, Kazakhstan, Belarus, Armenia) may work without a permit on the same basis as Kyrgyz citizens.

Citizens of Russia, Kazakhstan, Belarus and Armenia — no work permit required.
03.2

Freelance & Remote Work

If you work remotely for a foreign employer, see the Digital Nomad status guide. To register a company, read the LLC guide.

03.3

Studying in Kyrgyzstan

International students can enroll in public or private universities in KR. Full-time study requires a student visa or residence permit on educational grounds. Most universities offer instruction in Russian.

03.4

Digital Nomad status

Since 2025 Kyrgyzstan has a permanent Digital Nomad status (Cabinet Resolution No. 241 of 30 April 2025) for remote professionals in IT, creative and other digital fields. It is granted for 60 days, then extended for one year and renewed annually for up to 10 years. You apply online via the Government Services portal; according to Economist.kg, no additional work permit is needed. Full guide.
