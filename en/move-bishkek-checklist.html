Moving to Bishkek Checklist 2026: Everything to Do Before and After Arrival

Complete pre-arrival and post-arrival checklist for moving to Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan. Practical steps and timeline.

This article was written before we started dating every fact. Rules changed in 2026, so check details against sources. Verified answers by situation: guides.

What should I do before moving to Bishkek?

Key pre-arrival steps: check visa requirements for your nationality, arrange travel insurance, book first accommodation (hotel or Airbnb for first 2 weeks), download offline maps (Maps.me), save emergency numbers (police: 102, ambulance: 103), join expat Facebook/Telegram groups for advice.

What are the first things to do after arriving in Bishkek?

First-week priorities: buy a local SIM card (airport or carrier shop), check how long you may stay without registration (30 days for US, EU and EAEU citizens) and agree registration with your landlord, find long-term housing, open a bank account and install a taxi app.

Where should I start when preparing to move to Bishkek?

Start by arranging a visa or confirming your visa-free eligibility. Simultaneously look for accommodation through Telegram groups and OLX.kg. Before moving it is advisable to arrange health insurance and have savings covering at least 2–3 months of living expenses.

How can I find an apartment in Bishkek before arriving?

Best resources are OLX.kg, Diesel.elcat.kg and relevant Telegram groups such as "Аренда Бишкек для экспатов". Request photo and video tours from landlords via messaging apps. It is advisable to book short-term serviced apartments for the first 2–4 weeks and find a long-term rental after arriving.

What should I do in the first days after arriving in Bishkek?

Check how long you may stay without registration: 30 days for citizens of the USA, EU, UK and EAEU (since September 2026). Your landlord files registration at a public service center or online. Buy a local SIM card (Beeline, MegaCom or O!), open a bank account, install a taxi app, find nearby shops and pharmacies.

Can I bring my pet when moving to Kyrgyzstan?

Yes, pets can be brought in with a veterinary passport showing vaccination records (especially rabies) and a health certificate issued no more than 5 days before travel. Quarantine is generally not required. Check specific rules with your airline.

What items are best to bring with you when moving to Bishkek?

It is advisable to bring: a supply of familiar medications (some brands are harder to find), quality winter clothing (winters are cold), a laptop and all professional accessories. Appliances and furniture are better purchased locally — cheaper and easier than shipping.

Do Russian and Kazakh citizens need an international passport to enter Kyrgyzstan?

No, Russian and Kazakh citizens can enter Kyrgyzstan with their domestic (national) passport. The same applies to other EAEU citizens from Belarus and Armenia. Having an international passport is still more convenient for onward travel.

How to set up mobile banking immediately after moving to Bishkek?

The fastest way is to open an account with Mbank: a basic account can be created through the app with a Kyrgyz phone number. For full functionality a branch visit with passport is required. A Mbank account is usually opened within the first 1–2 days of arrival.

What vaccinations are recommended before moving to Kyrgyzstan?

The WHO recommends standard vaccinations (measles, hepatitis A and B, tetanus, diphtheria) for travellers to Central Asia. For trips to mountain and remote areas a rabies vaccination should be considered. No mandatory vaccinations are required for entry into Kyrgyzstan.

How to find the expat community in Bishkek?

Main resources are Telegram groups "Expats in Bishkek" and "Digital Nomads Kyrgyzstan" and the Facebook group "Expats in Bishkek". Coworking spaces and language exchanges (Language Exchange Bishkek) are good places to meet people. Meetups are held regularly.

Is it worth hiring a relocation agency for moving to Bishkek?

For a straightforward move to Bishkek an agency is not necessary — most matters can be handled independently. However when relocating with a family, needing a residence permit or starting a business, the help of a lawyer or agent will save time and stress.
