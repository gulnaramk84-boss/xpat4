04 · Business, Housing & More

Practical Life in Kyrgyzstan

Company registration, housing, banking, taxes and everything else you need to know for a comfortable life in Kyrgyzstan.

04.1

Setting Up a Business

Foreign individuals and companies can be founders of an LLC (OsOO). Registration goes through a one-stop shop; the standard time is 3–5 working days, and online it can be faster. We have no verified data yet on a foreigner registering as a sole proprietor or on the state fee: details in the guide.

04.2

Renting Housing in Bishkek

Bishkek's rental market is active. Foreigners should sign an official lease agreement, which is required for registration.

04.3

Banking

Conditions for a non-resident to open an account depend on the bank, so ask in advance. Large banks include KICB, Optima Bank, RSK and Bakai Bank. A fee for accrediting accounts of foreign companies is being prepared: what we know.

04.4

Daily Life

Bishkek is a relatively affordable and comfortable city. Good infrastructure, many cafes and co-working spaces. Mobile internet is widely available; a SIM card can be obtained with a passport. Official languages are Kyrgyz and Russian.
