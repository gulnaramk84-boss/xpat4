My country needs a visa: how to get a Kyrgyzstan e-visa

Who needs a visa, how to apply on the Foreign Ministry portal, how long review takes and what to do if the application is sent back. Based on the official evisa.e-gov.kg portal.

Published November 24, 2026. Checked November 24, 2026.
Report a mistake
In short
  • Apply only on the Foreign Ministry portal evisa.e-gov.kg, in English. An e-visa gives the same right of entry as a visa sticker in the passport.
  • Review takes 5 working days from the moment all documents are in. When applying you can choose review within 2 working days.
  • The fee is not refunded after a refusal or if you never use the visa. Buy tickets after approval.
  • We did not find the fee on the portal's open pages. You can enter only through certain border crossings.

Do you need a visa

First check whether you need a visa at all. Citizens of many countries can enter without one: periods by country are in our table. The official check is on the portal: Information → Country information, then pick the country that issued your passport.

You need a visa if your country has no visa-free entry, for example Chinese citizens with an ordinary passport travelling outside a tour group. To stay longer than the visa-free period you also need a visa or another document, such as a residence permit. A visa run does not reset the count.

If you fly through and do not leave the airport transit area, you do not need a visa. The portal's FAQ says so.

Which visa fits

The portal describes more than a dozen visa types. For trips these are the most common:

  • Tourist (type TS): for tourism, for up to 30 days.
  • Business (type B): up to 90 days for meetings, conferences, sports events and medical treatment at an organisation's invitation. It does not allow work or running a business.
  • Transit (type TR): up to 3 days to travel through Kyrgyzstan to another country.
  • To work you need the Uniform permit rather than a visa: it combines the work visa and the work permit. See the work guide.

There are other types too: study, visiting relatives, Digital nomad (guide), and for family members of a foreigner. Their terms are on the portal under Information on visa categories and types.

How to apply

  1. Open evisa.e-gov.kg and choose Apply for a visa. Enter the country of your passport, the passport type and the service: standard, or review within 2 working days.
  2. Fill in the form in English. You need a valid passport, a photo and a bank card. Depending on the visa type, other documents may be needed; they must be translated into Kyrgyz or Russian.
  3. Confirm the application with the code from the email within 72 hours, or it is cancelled and you start again. If no email arrives, check spam.
  4. Check everything at the preview step. Nothing can be changed after payment.
  5. Pay by card. It does not have to be in your name, but it must be enabled for international payments.

Each traveller needs their own e-visa, including children, even if they are entered in a parent's passport.

Timing and money

Review takes 5 working days, counted from the moment all required documents are in. On the price the portal says only that it depends on the category, length and number of entries; you see the amount when you apply.

Payment is not refunded after a refusal, after a mistake in your details, or if you never use the visa. Mistakes in the form lead to refusal, so check everything against your passport. The portal advises buying flights only after approval.

Visa validity and length of stay are different things. Validity is the window in which you can enter. Length of stay is how many days you may spend in the country within it.

Where you can enter

You cannot enter on an e-visa through every border crossing. A Foreign Ministry page we opened in a copy from 12 July 2024 lists twelve: Manas and Osh airports and the road crossings Chon-Kapka, Chaldybar, Ak-Zhol, Ak-Tilek, Dostuk, Kyzyl-Bel, Kulundu, Bor-Dobo, Irkeshtam and Torugart. We found no newer list: the ministry site was down on 24 November 2026. If you travel overland, check your crossing first.

From 25 November 2026 Chaldybar is closed to vehicles (news).

Once the visa is issued, the portal gives a link where you can print it. The printout has a QR code that can be read with a phone.

If the application is sent back

A returned application is not a refusal, and the visa fee is not lost. If something needs fixing, more documents or a further payment, you get an email. Go to the portal, choose Check status and enter your reference number. Fields to fix are marked red: correct them and press Submit. The 5 working days restart once everything is in.

Portal support phones: +996 703 428 865 and +996 999 312 002.

If your stay has already run out, an exit visa (type L) for up to 10 days is issued so you can leave. See the overstay guide.

What we did not find

  • The fee. It is not on the portal's open pages. Figures on agency sites differ, and they are the agencies' prices, not the state fee.
  • A list of entry points newer than July 2024.
  • Whether an e-visa can be extended inside the country. The portal says nothing about it.
  • Whether you must show the visa on paper. The ministry says it can be printed, but not that this is required.
