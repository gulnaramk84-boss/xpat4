How to Open a Company (OsOO) in Kyrgyzstan as a Foreigner 2026

Step-by-step guide to registering an OsOO (LLC equivalent) or sole trader (IP) in Kyrgyzstan. Costs, taxes and timelines.

This article was written before we started dating every fact. Rules changed in 2026, so check details against sources. Verified answers by situation: guides.

Can a foreigner open a company in Kyrgyzstan?

Yes. Foreigners can fully own and operate a company in Kyrgyzstan. The most common form is OsOO (Общество с ограниченной ответственностью), equivalent to an LLC. There is no minimum capital requirement.

How long does it take to register a company in Kyrgyzstan?

Company registration in Kyrgyzstan can be completed in 1 business day through the State Registration Service. Minimum fee is under $10.

What taxes does a company pay in Kyrgyzstan?

Standard rates on the general regime: profit tax 10% (Art. 240) and VAT 12% (Art. 254). Small businesses can use the single tax on revenue instead of profit tax and VAT: for services 4% for non-cash and 6% for cash payments, lower for some activities (Tax Code Art. 423, edition as of November 2026).

What is the difference between OsOO and IP in Kyrgyzstan?

OsOO is a limited liability company — the owner's personal assets are protected. IP (Individual Entrepreneur / sole trader) is simpler and cheaper to register but the owner bears unlimited personal liability.

Is an accountant required when opening an LLC in Kyrgyzstan?

An accountant is not required for registration, but is necessary for tax accounting and filing declarations. Small companies on the simplified tax system can use a part-time accountant (from 3,000–5,000 KGS/month). A full-time accountant is needed for high turnover or larger teams.

Which industries require special licences for foreign business in Kyrgyzstan?

Licences are required in: financial services (banks, MFOs, insurance), telecommunications, natural resource extraction, healthcare, education, alcohol and tobacco, and media. For foreign companies additional restrictions may apply in strategic sectors.

How do you close an LLC in Kyrgyzstan if the business did not work out?

Liquidating an LLC involves: a founders' resolution, publication of a notice, a tax audit and deregistration. The process takes 3–6 months. With no debts or tax violations the procedure is straightforward. Engaging a lawyer is recommended.

Can an LLC be renamed after registration in Kyrgyzstan?

Yes, renaming an LLC is possible by amending the charter documents. A founders' resolution, notarised charter amendments and re-registration with the State Registry are required. The process takes 3–10 working days.

Is share capital required when registering an LLC in Kyrgyzstan?

There is no minimum share capital requirement for an LLC in Kyrgyzstan. The amount of share capital is stated in the charter and can be nominal (for example, 1,000 KGS). This makes registration accessible for startups and small businesses without large initial investment.

Can a foreigner be the sole founder of an LLC in Kyrgyzstan?

Yes, a foreign national may be the sole founder of an LLC in Kyrgyzstan. A local partner is not required except for certain licensed activity types. The director may also be a foreigner.

What taxes does an LLC pay in Kyrgyzstan?

Main taxes for an LLC: profit tax 10%, VAT 12% on the general regime, and social contributions on the payroll. Small companies can switch to the single tax on revenue instead of profit tax and VAT: for services 4% for non-cash and 6% for cash payments. Check the contribution rates with the Social Fund.

Is a physical office required to register a company in Kyrgyzstan?

An LLC registration requires a legal address. This can be an actual office, a coworking space offering a legal address service or the founder's address. There is no minimum office space requirement. Tax authorities may verify actual presence at the address during audits.

Can I manage a Kyrgyz company remotely from abroad?

Yes, many foreign entrepreneurs manage Kyrgyz companies remotely by appointing a local director or accountant. Tax filings can be submitted online. However, opening bank accounts and notarial procedures may require physical presence or a notarised power of attorney.
