01 · Visa & Entry

Entering Kyrgyzstan

Visa types, visa-free entry, border crossing rules and registration at your place of stay.

01.1

Visa-Free Entry

Citizens of many countries can enter Kyrgyzstan without a visa. The visa-free stay depends on nationality, so check it at evisa.e-gov.kg. Since 23 September 2026 the registration-free period for citizens of several countries is 30 days: what it means.

EU — 30 days USA — 60 days China — by agreement
01.2

Visa on Arrival (VOA)

Some nationalities can obtain a visa on arrival at Manas Airport (Bishkek). It is issued on-site; cost depends on type and duration. Check the current list of eligible countries in advance at evisa.e-gov.kg.

01.3

Electronic Visa (e-Visa)

Since 2017, citizens of any country can apply for a tourist or business e-Visa online at evisa.e-gov.kg. Valid for 30 or 60 days. Entry is only through Manas or Osh airports and select land border crossings.

01.4

Registration at your place of stay

If you stay longer than the registration-free period, you must register at the address where you live. Since 23 September 2026 this period is 30 days for citizens of the USA, EU, UK, Japan, South Korea and several other countries. File no later than its last day. Full guide.

01.5

Extension & Visa Run

Leaving and re-entering (a 'visa run') resets the stay counter, but immigration authorities monitor frequent border crossings. For long-term residence, obtaining a residence permit (VNJ) is recommended.
