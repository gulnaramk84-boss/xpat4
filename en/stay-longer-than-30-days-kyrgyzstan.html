Arrived visa-free and want to stay longer: what to do about registration

How many days you can live without registration, when it stops being valid and what a violation costs. Under the procedure in force since 23 September 2026.

Published November 19, 2026. Checked November 22, 2026.
Report a mistake
In short
  • Since 23 September 2026 the registration-free period for citizens of the USA, EU, UK, Japan, Korea and several others is 30 days instead of 60.
  • Register after entering and no later than the last day of that period.
  • Leaving for 30 days or more cancels the registration; you need a new one on return.
  • Without documents for your stay the fine under Article 431 is 55 to 200 calculation indices.

How many days without registration

All periods by country, visa-free and registration-free, are in one table.

Visa-free entry and registration are different things. The visa-free period says how long you may be in the country. The registration-free period says when you must register. Since 23 September 2026 the registration-free period for citizens of several countries was cut from 60 to 30 days.

The list includes citizens of Australia, Austria, Belgium, the United Kingdom, Germany, Greece, Denmark, Spain, Italy, Canada, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, the Republic of Korea, Singapore, the USA, Finland, France, Switzerland, Sweden and Japan. One source adds “and other states”, the other does not. For citizens of Russia, Kazakhstan, Belarus and Armenia, for.kg (a Sputnik piece) gives a control period of 30 calendar days. If your country is not on the list, check the period with Kyzmat before counting days yourself.

When to file

Registration is done after entry and no later than the last day you may stay without it. The registration slip confirms registration at the address and for the period shown on it. It does not replace a visa or a residence permit.

We cover how to register when you rent a flat in a separate guide.

When registration stops being valid

  • You left Kyrgyzstan for 30 calendar days or more: the registration becomes invalid on its own. Register again when you return.
  • Your address, passport, personal data, or the period or purpose of stay changed: re-register within 10 working days.
  • EAEU citizens on a first employment contract not yet confirmed in the tax service database get registration for up to 60 days, and after confirmation for up to one year.

If you need more than the visa-free period

Registration does not extend the visa-free period. A long stay needs another basis: an e-visa (what it is), a residence permit (what we know) or, for those who work for a foreign employer, the Digital Nomad status (guide). If the time has already run out, read the overstay guide.

What a violation costs

If a foreigner has no documents for staying, including registration, the fine under Article 431 of the Code of Offences depends on duration: up to one month, 55 calculation indices; one to three months, 100; three to six months, 150; six months to a year, 175; over a year, 200. According to press reports one index equals 100 som, so 5,500 to 20,000 som.

We checked the text of Article 431 in the Ministry of Justice database on 19 November 2026. We read the registration resolution No. 600 only as retold by news outlets, so check the country list and periods with Kyzmat.
